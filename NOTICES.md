## License Acceptance

Per Community Specification License 1.0 §2.1.3.3, Licensees may indicate their
acceptance of the Community Specification License by issuing a pull request to
this file, including the Licensee's name, authorized individual and system
identifier (e.g. GitHub ID), and the specification version.

A Licensee may consent to the current version or any future version of the
Community Specification License by writing "or later" after the specification
version.

Acceptance is **not** required in order to implement DSX under the copyright
licence. It is how a Licensee comes within the patent grants of §2.1 — both the
grant from Contributors and the reciprocal grant from other Licensees under
§2.1.2. An implementer who ships the licence text with their distribution
(§2.1.3.1, §2.1.3.2) is equally a Licensee without appearing here.

## Patent Exclusions

Per Community Specification License 1.0 §3, a Contributor may exclude Necessary
Claims from its licensing commitments by filing an Exclusion Notice here, within
45 days of the contribution concerned. An Exclusion Notice must identify the
Draft Specification by name and the patent number(s) or, for unpublished
applications, either the filed text or the specific parts of the specification
whose implementation makes the claim necessary.

Claims not listed in an Exclusion Notice remain subject to the licensing
commitment.
